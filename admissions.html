Frequently asked questions

Admissions Answers

Is Friendship High a boarding school?

No. Friendship High is a government day school.

Which Forms can I apply for?

Entry is available across Forms 1–6, subject to placement and availability.

What documents do I need?

Requirements depend on the learner’s entry point. Review the draft requirements above and contact the school to confirm what is currently required.

How does placement work?

Applications and enquiries are reviewed through the school’s placement process. Applying does not guarantee placement.

What are the school hours?

School hours are 07:30–16:00.

What are the school fees?

Contact the school for current fee information.

How can I get help with an application?

Call +263 (242) 855158, email friendshiphighs@gmail.com, or use the Contact page.
