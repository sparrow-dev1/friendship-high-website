Who we are

A Place to Learn, Grow and Belong

Friendship High serves learners from Forms 1–6, offering pathways across Sciences, Commercials and Arts at Ordinary and Advanced Level.

Academic learning is supported by technology, sport, culture and opportunities for personal development within the wider school community.

Our story

Built Through Friendship

Friendship High grew from cooperation focused on expanding access to public high school education in the growing Hatcliffe community.

  1. May 2016Construction commencedThe Zimbabwean and Chinese governments worked together on the project, with China State Construction Engineering Corporation involved in its delivery.
  2. December 2017School handed overThe completed Friendship High campus was officially handed over to Zimbabwean authorities.
  3. 2018Learning beganThe new public high school became operational for the Hatcliffe community.
  4. January 2024Cultural exchange hostedFriendship High welcomed a China–Zimbabwe cultural exchange programme for learners.
